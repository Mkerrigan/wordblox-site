WordBlox Privacy Policy

Last updated 27 October 2026

WordBlox is a daily word puzzle for iPhone, made by Michael Kerrigan ("I", "me"). It has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. This page explains the small amount of data the app does handle.

On your device only

Your puzzle results, streak, theme progress and settings are stored on your iPhone, shared only with the WordBlox home screen widget. They are never sent to me. Deleting the app deletes them.

Anonymous usage analytics

To learn whether people enjoy the puzzle enough to come back, the app sends anonymous usage events to TelemetryDeck, a privacy-focused analytics service based in the EU:

EventWhat it includes
A puzzle started or finishedDaily, archive or practice; difficulty; theme; words found; slips; hints used; score; streak length
The Premium screen shown, or a purchase madeWhich product was bought (never payment details)
App sessionsWhen the app was opened and for how long

Each event also includes the number of days since you first played, your device model, iOS version, app version and language, and a random identifier for this installation. The identifier is a salted, one-way hash created on your device; it cannot be used to identify you, is not shared with other apps, and changes if you reinstall. No names, email addresses, contacts, locations, IP addresses or advertising identifiers are collected. The data is not linked to your identity, not used for advertising and not sold.

Apple services

  • Purchases are handled entirely by Apple. I receive no payment details. See Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Game Center: if you are signed in, your score for each day's puzzle is posted to the daily leaderboard under your Game Center nickname. Game Center is operated by Apple; you can sign out in Settings.
  • Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone. Nothing is sent to a server. You can turn them off in Settings.

Children

WordBlox is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your choices

Because the analytics data is anonymous and not linked to you, I cannot look up or delete a particular person's events. The puzzle works fully offline, and events are only sent when your iPhone has a connection.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
